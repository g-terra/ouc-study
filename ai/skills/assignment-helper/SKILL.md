_Paths: `<sem>` = the current semester folder (named in STUDENT.md; otherwise the newest folder under `semesters/`)._

# Assignment helper

1. Find the brief: the deadlines (app dashboard, or `<sem>/_meta/eclass-meta.json`) → the course folder (`assignment-files/` holds the PDFs/DOCX) and the text copy in `<sem>/text/`. Also check `<sem>/_meta/eclass-meta.json` for the full description.
2. Break it down: the question asked, what's being assessed, which weeks/files are relevant, the expected length/format, and the due date (days left).
3. Give a plan: an outline with headings, the evidence and concepts to use (cite course material, then research via **research-topic** if needed), and a time-boxed checklist that is added to the current `<sem>/plans/` file.
4. When they share a draft: give feedback on accuracy, completeness against the brief, structure, citations and clarity. Suggest improvements, but don't rewrite it into a submittable answer.
5. Integrity: no ghost-writing. COS514 explicitly forbids copy-pasting tutorial answers.
6. When they say it's submitted, update the deadlines (app dashboard, or `<sem>/_meta/eclass-meta.json`) status and `memory/progress.md`.
