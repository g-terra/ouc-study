# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be welcoming and respectful.** People here study, work and live in different places and languages, at different levels of experience. Assume good intent.
- **Be constructive.** Critique ideas and code, not people. Accept feedback gracefully.
- **No harassment.** No insults, trolling, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, or sharing anyone's private information (including their eClass data, grades or submissions) without permission.
- **Respect academic integrity.** Don't use this project to help anyone cheat, and don't share course materials you don't own.

These rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@g-terra](https://github.com/g-terra) (or through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/g-terra/ouc-study/security/advisories/new) if you can't reach them otherwise). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
